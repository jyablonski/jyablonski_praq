# The State of AI — November 2026

**Coverage:** November 1–27, 2026 (month-to-date draft)\
**Sources checked through:** November 27, 2026 (Pacific time)

## The month in brief

November strengthened the capability case from the August essay. A Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann hypothesis passed independent formal verification, and two premium model upgrades came with lower list prices. Anthropic’s IPO prospectus disclosed roughly $320 billion in fixed, determinable contractual obligations as of September 30. The open question is how much recurring customer value will support those commitments. [August essay](aug_2026.md), [Lean and Mathlib verification](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/), [Anthropic launch](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2), [OpenAI launch](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/), [Anthropic prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/anthropicpbc-424b4.htm).

## Models, products, and tooling

- **Nov 4–24 · Open weights at several scales.** Tencent announced its multimodal Hy4 flagship on Nov 4 and made the weights available later in the month under the Tencent Hy License. Mistral now lists Large 4 as generally available after its October weight release. Its license requires a separate agreement for commercial model-as-a-service use. At the local end, PrismML released the Apache 2.0 Ternary Bonsai 2.5 27B on Nov 24 and reports a roughly 6 GB weight footprint. Actual inference memory also depends on the runtime and context length. [Tencent launch](https://www.tencent.com/tencent-launches-hy4-joining-the-ranks-of-the-worlds-leading-open-source-ai-models/), [Hy4 repository](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hy4), [Mistral model docs](https://docs.mistral.ai/models/mistral-large-4-0), [Mistral weights and license](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/), [PrismML release](https://prismml.com/news/prismml-launches-ternary-bonsai-2-5-27b).
- **Nov 4 · OpenAI released Images 2.6.** The API offers Sunburst for detailed work and Flare for fast iteration, plus SVG and animated GIF output. Markup-guided editing lets users indicate where to apply a change. [OpenAI API changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog), [image generation guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-generation).
- **Nov 9 · Anthropic released Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2.** Both use the same underlying model with different safeguards and access. Fable is available through the Claude Platform API and major clouds. Anthropic offers Mythos access through its verification programs and makes it available within Claude Security for enterprise customers. [Anthropic release and availability](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2).
- **Nov 10–18 · Google expanded Gemini 4 and Alibaba launched Qwen4-Max.** Google widened Carbon access on Nov 10, launched Hydrogen for fast everyday agent work on Nov 12, and began rolling out Neon on Nov 18 as a middle option for complex work where latency matters. Alibaba released Qwen4-Max on Nov 16 with text, image, and video understanding. [Google Carbon](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/), [Google Hydrogen](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/), [Google Neon](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/), [Alibaba launch](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/press-room/alibaba-unveils-qwen4-max).
- **Nov 17–23 · OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol.** Both reached the API, ChatGPT Work, and Codex. Astra targets demanding reasoning and coding, while Sol targets everyday agent workloads. A chat-optimized GPT-6.2 build began rolling out on Nov 23. For API integrations, tool calling for Astra and Sol uses the Responses API. [OpenAI launch](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/), [API changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog), [ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes).
- **Nov 18–19 · Genie 4 moved beyond the October research preview.** Google brought its interactive world model to eligible AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie, then opened developer access through the Gemini API and AI Studio. Generated environments support live controls and synchronized audio. [Project Genie rollout](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/), [Gemini API launch](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/genie-4-in-gemini-api/).
- **Nov 23 · Claude Ship extended agents into pull-request review and approval.** On GitHub repositories connected to Claude Team or Enterprise, Code Review is generally available. PR Steward and Claude Approvals are in beta and run under repository settings and team approval policies. [Anthropic product announcement](https://claude.com/resources/articles/introducing-claude-ship).

## Research, discoveries, and evaluation

- **Nov 3 · Mistral published its Large 4 training report.** During post-training, the team rewarded shorter correct responses within each group of attempts, then distilled several reasoning-effort settings into one model. It’s a concrete example of tuning inference cost during training. Mistral reports the results from its own run. [Mistral technical report](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.mistral-large-4).
- **Nov 5 and 18 · Two reports measured AI-assisted AI research.** In its external review of Anthropic’s internal data, METR estimated that growth in Anthropic’s internal capability index accelerated by roughly 1.9× under its main specification, with limited visibility into how much of the acceleration came from AI assistance. OpenAI’s internal analysis estimated that zero-intervention success rose from 23% to 46% between March and October for delegated research tasks estimated to take a human two to four hours. The kinds of tasks researchers delegate may have changed over the period. These are measurements of different outcomes at two labs. [METR’s Anthropic assessment](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/), [OpenAI’s research report](https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-jagged-frontier/).
- **Nov 10 · Google DeepMind used agents to verify selected Signal properties.** AlphaProof Code split the work between experts who reviewed the formal specifications and agents that generated machine-checked Lean proofs for properties of the compiled `libsignal` implementation. The result applies to the implementation and properties covered by those specifications. [DeepMind announcement](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/), [technical paper](https://deepmind.google/documents/36/Formal_Verification_of_Signal_4.pdf).
- **Nov 20 · METR expanded its public capability evaluation.** Adding the harder TH2 suite lowered the estimated task-completion horizon for a given model compared with TH1.1 alone. The combined suite’s historical trend showed a roughly 3.6-month doubling time, while a separate task-level capability index gave weak evidence of a change in the rate of public-frontier progress. That makes the task suite and metric part of any month-to-month comparison. [METR’s methods and results](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-20-q4-2026-capability-update/).
- **Nov 20–21 · A Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann hypothesis passed independent checks.** Levent Alpöge released the formal proof on Nov 20. The Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative independently checked the statement and proof the following day. The verification answers the formal correctness question. A readable account of the mathematical ideas is a separate piece of work. [Alpöge’s announcement](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf), [Lean and Mathlib verification](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/).

## Infrastructure, financing, and adoption

- **Nov 9 · Small decisions accounted for a large share of requests on one gateway.** In its November report on October traffic, Vercel said decision models that classify, score, or select options handled 26% of AI Gateway requests and 0.53% of spend. The figures come from Vercel’s routed traffic. They illustrate how request volume can climb quickly in a workload with low spend per call. [Vercel AI Gateway Production Index](https://vercel.com/blog/ai-gateway-production-index-november-2026).
- **Nov 16 · PJM proposed new grid requirements for large computing loads.** Its filing at FERC would require these facilities to stay connected through specified voltage and frequency disturbances. The proposal adds a power-system reliability requirement to the list of constraints on getting large sites online. [PJM filing summary](https://insidelines.pjm.com/pjm-proposes-new-rules-to-protect-grid-reliability-as-data-center-demand-grows/).
- **Nov 17 · NVIDIA reported $107.6 billion in Data Center revenue.** That was up 110% year over year for its fiscal quarter ended Oct 25. It measures revenue at the hardware supplier. Facility owners and model providers have their own cost and return profiles. [NVIDIA quarterly results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/).
- **Nov 19–20 · Anthropic went public and disclosed the scale of its compute exposure.** It began trading on Nasdaq Nov 19 and reported $91.6 billion in gross proceeds to the company from the IPO at closing, with a separate $26.0 billion concurrent private placement. Its prospectus listed approximately $320.0 billion in fixed, determinable contractual obligations as of Sept 30, mainly purchase commitments for cloud hosting and third-party infrastructure. The filing also describes equipment leases through special-purpose subsidiaries, with parent guarantees and cross-default provisions. Contractual obligations and proceeds have different time horizons. The filing gives a way to track how capacity delivery and usage translate into cash outflows over time. [Anthropic IPO closing](https://www.anthropic.com/news/ipo-close), [SEC prospectus](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/anthropicpbc-424b4.htm).

## Policy, safety, and labor

- **Nov 19 · U.S. controls extended to certain remote access arrangements for advanced compute.** A Bureau of Industry and Security interim final rule took effect on publication. It adds licensing controls for certain exports, reexports, or transfers involving data centers that provide significant compute access to covered entities, including in scenarios where the hosting data center is in a third country. [Federal Register rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-19/pdf/2026-23529.pdf).
- **November research · Early-career employment remains the signal to watch.** Stanford’s November update, using ADP payroll data from the same firms over time, reported that employment among 22- to 25-year-olds in the two most AI-exposed groups of jobs was nearly 4% below a year earlier. Exposure reflects the tasks AI could do. The payroll sample and that exposure measure leave open how much of the decline AI caused. A separate Nov 6 working paper on GitHub Copilot found lower technical hiring among the firms in its adoption study, concentrated in junior, coding-heavy roles. Separations showed no detectable effect in that study. [Stanford indicators update](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/app/uploads/2026/11/AIEI_MU_Nov26.pdf), [working paper on AI-assisted programming and labor demand](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2026-11/AILaborDemand_06Nov2026.pdf).

## Standing scorecard

| Question | Signal to track | This month and what would change the outlook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is the technology getting more useful? | Reliability on real workloads and the open-weight gap in coding and agent tasks | **Stronger evidence in formally checkable work.** The Lean results provide concrete examples. Test open and closed models on the same repository tasks with a fixed harness, time budget, and acceptance criteria to gauge how much of the gap matters in practice. |
| Can the investment earn attractive returns? | Inference volume and spend versus prices, enterprise ROI, hardware life and resale values, financing stress, and power constraints | **Strong hardware sales and clearer financing exposure.** Lower model rates and cheap decision calls make traffic a poor standalone proxy for revenue. Watch paid usage and spend together, then compare recurring cash generation with capacity payments as delivery ramps. |
| Is the transition socially manageable? | Hiring, wages, reemployment, productivity, and who bears the costs | **Early-career hiring is the clearest concern in this edition.** Track hires and separations by age and occupation, then follow wages and reemployment to see whether the slowdown changes career prospects. |
