# Cash Investing Notes: HYSA vs CDs vs T-Bills

*Last updated: Oct 2026. Rates change; re-check before acting.*

## Quick Comparison (per $100k, 1 year, CA 9.3% bracket)

| Option | Rate | Interest | CA Tax | After CA Tax | Access |
| -------------- | ------ | -------- | ------ | ------------ | --------------------------- |
| 52-week T-bill | 4.616% | $4,616 | $0 | $4,616 | Sell any business day (T+1) |
| Ally CD | 3.35% | $3,350 | $312 | $3,038 | Early withdrawal penalty |
| Ally HYSA | 3.10% | $3,100 | $288 | $2,812 | Instant |

Federal tax applies equally to all three, so it doesn't change the ranking.

Rule of thumb: in California, a CD or HYSA needs ~5.1% to match a 4.6% T-bill after state tax.

## Ally HYSA

What it is: FDIC-insured savings, variable rate, instant access.

Strategy:

- Hold the emergency fund here (6 to 12 months of expenses).
- Keep total Ally deposits under $250k (FDIC limit per depositor, per ownership category).
- Don't park long-term money here: lowest rate and fully taxed by CA.
- No state tax advantage (fully taxed by CA).

## CDs

What it is: FDIC-insured, fixed rate for a fixed term, penalty to withdraw early. Allows you to lock in a rate for the term.

Strategy:

- Generally skip them while T-bills pay more after tax.
- Existing CD: don't break it automatically. Compare the early withdrawal penalty vs the T-bill rate gain over the remaining term. If it matures soon, let it run and move the proceeds to T-bills.
- Brokered CDs on Fidelity: only worth it if the rate beats ~5.1% (CA-taxable).
- CDs count toward the $250k FDIC limit at the same bank.
- No state tax advantage (fully taxed by CA).

## T-Bills (Fidelity)

What it is: Short-term US government debt. Bought at a discount, paid face value at maturity. You get nothing until maturity. The difference is you typically get paid out more than a comparable CD or HYSA after taxes.

Example (Sep 29, 2026 auction): $10,000 bill cost $9,555.11, pays $10,000 in one year = $444.89 earned (4.616%).

Why it wins when you don't need the money:

- Exempt from CA state tax (biggest edge for a Californian).
- Higher rate than Ally CD/HYSA right now.
- Backed directly by the US government, no $250k cap.
- Locks a rate for the term (52-week = one year).
- No fees buying new issues at Fidelity.
- Tax advantage: exempt from California state tax. Federal tax applies as ordinary income in the year the bill matures.

Strategy:

- Use for money not needed for 1+ year (house fund).
- Stick to 26-week or 52-week bills.
- Split purchases across multiple auctions so not everything locks on one day's rate.
- Hold in the Individual - TOD brokerage account, kept only for T-bills (no auto-pays).
- Check Auto Roll on every order.

### How to Buy

1. Fixed Income > Bonds & CDs > New Issues > Treasury.
1. Bills appear after the Treasury announces them (52-week: auction every 4 weeks).
1. Enter quantity in $1,000s (50 = $50,000). Confirm cash is "available to trade."
1. Check Auto Roll, submit before auction morning.
1. Rate is set by the auction (noncompetitive bid).

### Auto Roll

- At maturity, Fidelity buys a new bill of the same term and face value automatically.
- Interest lands in core cash (SPAXX); sweep it to SGOV or the next bill 1 to 2x per year.
- Each roll gets the market rate at that time, not the original rate.
- Cancel any time before the replacement order fills (reminder email 7 to 10 days before maturity).
- Core cash can briefly show negative between auction and settlement. Normal.

### Getting Money Out Early

- Can't redeem early with the Treasury, but can sell on Fidelity's secondary market any business day.
- You get roughly purchase price + interest earned so far, minus a small spread.
- If rates rose since purchase, slightly less (e.g. +1% rates with 6 months left = ~$250 on $50k).
- Realistic worst case: give up some interest, not principal.

### Taxes

- Federal: ordinary income in the year the bill matures (bills bought late 2026 hit 2027).
- California: $0. Subtract it on Schedule CA (tax software handles it).

## The Plan

1. Emergency fund stays in Ally HYSA.
1. Move the rest to Fidelity Individual - TOD in chunks.
1. Buy 52-week (or 26-week) T-bills across several auctions, Auto Roll on.
1. Sweep interest from SPAXX periodically.
1. Before buying the house: turn off Auto Roll, let bills mature to cash (or sell early).
